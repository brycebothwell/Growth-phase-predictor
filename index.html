Research use only — not clinically validated. The phase engine uses synthetic, literature-informed growth curves, not a model trained on thousands of children. Displayed percentages are uncalibrated model weights, not probabilities of being correct. The male PHV-age prior incorporates a published Oregon Growth Study summary (51 boys, mean age 13.54 years, SD 1.12), but this is NOT training on individual records and does NOT validate the model. No patient data are uploaded; calculations run locally in your browser.

Patient observations

Enter at least 3 measurement dates. Six-month spacing is generally more reliable for interval growth velocity than very short intervals. Every additional valid measurement can update the estimate.

Cephalometric history (new)

Enter measurements from one existing lateral ceph and optionally add earlier or later cephalograms. Use consistent landmark definitions and magnification correction. No additional radiographs should be taken solely for this research prototype.

Co–Gn = effective mandibular length; Co–Go = ramus height; mandibular plane angle is optional. Date each observation.

Growth rates reflect retrospectively measured intervals, not untreated growth predictions. Treatment, projection, landmark error, and differing time intervals can affect comparisons.

What the tool means

Two measurements yield one interval-average velocity. Three can suggest acceleration or deceleration, but cannot reliably locate the peak. The reported phase concerns statural PHV, not mandibular growth or optimal appliance treatment timing. Do not enter names or other identifying details in shared devices.
