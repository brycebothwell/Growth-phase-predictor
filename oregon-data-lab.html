Published Oregon Growth Study reference · 51 boys

Grau (University of Oregon, 1977) analyzed serial height measurements from 54 boys, with 51 usable for the observed growth-peak analysis. Mean age at the midpoint of the six-month interval containing maximum height gain was 13.54 years (SD 1.12). The original thesis reported the following observed peak-age frequency distribution (Figure 2). This is actual Oregon summary data, not individual height trajectories, and should not be mistaken for a validated probability of PHV or for female reference values.

Next development stage

Once the actual Oregon stature measurements have been obtained, the next step will be training sex-specific longitudinal growth models, estimating reference peak-height-velocity from complete trajectories, and testing what a model can infer when only the first three visits are visible. Use participant-level held-out validation and independent cohorts before displaying calibrated PHV phase probabilities.
