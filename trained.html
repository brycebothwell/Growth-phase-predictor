Supported input range

The model uses up to seven most recent measurements within three years. Selected measurements must be from ages 8–17, approximately 2.4–13.2 months apart, spanning at least six months. Extra observations can be entered, but older observations outside this window are not used for prediction. More measurements do not guarantee a better estimate.

What was actually trained?

Separate male and female random-forest regressors use age, height and observed velocity features. They were trained on 101 eligible Berkeley and 71 eligible Harpenden participants, divided into training, calibration and testing groups by child. Only 102 participants contributed to fitted tree parameters. The previous version used manually specified curves and an Oregon summary prior; it was not Berkeley-trained.

Model v2 is an exploratory historical-cohort model. It has not had prospective clinical validation. Pubertal height timing is not a prediction of mandibular growth or treatment response.
